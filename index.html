01

Encode behaviour

Runtime signals are reduced to discrete player states so transition counts can be updated and queried without blocking the game loop.

02

Predict the next state

The Markov model estimates the most likely next encoded state from observed transitions. It does not claim to predict a human “mistake.”

03

Deduplicate frames

A state epoch prevents repeated render frames from counting the same prediction opportunity multiple times.

04

Prevent leakage

Predictions are frozen before the outcome is observed. Evaluation receives completed events through an adapter instead of training on future data.

05

Separate modes

Mode A evaluates a frozen model; Mode B evaluates online prequential learning. Keeping them separate makes results interpretable.
